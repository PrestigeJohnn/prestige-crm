# AI CRM - Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## General Questions

### Q: What is AI CRM?
A: AI CRM is a local desktop CRM application for managing customers, orders, activities, and more. It runs entirely on your computer with no cloud dependency.

### Q: What are the system requirements?
A: Windows 10 or later, 4GB RAM minimum, 200MB disk space.

### Q: Is my data stored in the cloud?
A: No. All data is stored locally on your computer in a SQLite database file.

### Q: I see a blank/white screen when starting the app.
A: This usually means the server is still loading. Wait 10-15 seconds. If the problem persists, try restarting the app.

## Data Management

### Q: How do I backup my data?
A: Go to Settings → Data & Backup → Backup Now. You can also enable auto-backup in settings.

### Q: How do I restore from a backup?
A: Go to Settings → Data & Backup → Restore Backup and select your backup file.

### Q: Can I import data from another CRM?
A: Yes. Go to Import/Export → Import Data and upload a CSV or Excel file.

### Q: How do I export my data?
A: Go to Import/Export → Export Data and choose the data type and format.

## Features

### Q: How do I create a new account?
A: Click "Accounts" in the sidebar, then click "+ New Account" button.

### Q: How do I track a deal?
A: Create an Opportunity and move it through stages (Discovery → Qualification → Proposal → Negotiation → Closed Won/Lost).

### Q: Can I schedule meetings?
A: Yes. Go to Meetings in the sidebar and create a new meeting with date, time, and attendees.

## Troubleshooting

### Q: The app is running slowly.
A: Try closing other applications to free up memory. If the database is very large, consider archiving old data.

### Q: I see an error message.
A: Note the error message and contact support. You can also check the logs in the `logs/` directory.
