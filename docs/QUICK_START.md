# AI CRM - Quick Start Guide

## 5 Minutes to Get Started

### Step 1: Launch the App
Double-click the AI CRM icon on your desktop. The app will start and show the login screen.

### Step 2: Log In
Use the default admin credentials:
- **Email:** Johnn@admin.com.sg
- **Password:** Johnn@123

### Step 3: Explore the Dashboard
After login, you'll see the Dashboard with:
- KPI cards showing key metrics
- Charts for orders and activities
- Recent accounts, orders, and activities

### Step 4: Add Your First Account
1. Click "Accounts" in the sidebar
2. Click "+ New Account"
3. Fill in the company details
4. Click "Save"

### Step 5: Create an Opportunity
1. Click "Opportunities" in the sidebar
2. Click "+ New Opportunity"
3. Fill in the deal details
4. Click "Save"

### Step 6: Log an Activity
1. Click "Activities" in the sidebar
2. Click "+ New Activity"
3. Choose the type (Call, Email, Meeting, etc.)
4. Fill in the details
5. Click "Save"

## Next Steps

- Use the search bar in the header to quickly find anything
